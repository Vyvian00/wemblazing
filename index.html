ABOUT ME

ENRICO
ESPOSTO

WEMBLAZING comes from the union of WE and EMBLAZING.

WE, because every project begins with two perspectives coming together: mine and the client’s. Design becomes a shared journey, shaped through dialogue, ideas and decisions made together.

EMBLAZING, because I believe in design that can leave a mark, giving form to an identity that feels distinctive and meaningful.

WEMBLAZING reflects the way I approach design: not simply creating for someone, but creating together something worth remembering.
