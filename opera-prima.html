ITALIAN GELATO · PREMIUM RETAIL CONCEPT
THE CONCEPT

Gelato as
an art form.

Opera Prima is a premium identity concept for an Italian gelateria built around restraint, craftsmanship and the ritual of serving gelato.

The visual system treats the spoon as both object and symbol. Its silhouette becomes part of the monogram, while fine circular geometry creates a language that can expand from signage to packaging and editorial applications.

01 / IDENTITY SYSTEMOPERA PRIMA · GELATO ITALIANO

The primary signature combines an elegant high-contrast wordmark with generous spacing, creating a restrained visual language suited to a contemporary Italian gelateria.

The OP monogram condenses the concept into a compact mark. The spoon becomes the vertical axis of the symbol, connecting the initials directly to the product and the act of serving.

02 / COLOR SYSTEM
ESPRESSO BLACK #211B18

Primary brand colour. Used for packaging, menus and dark identity surfaces.

WARM IVORY #E8E3DE

Core light neutral used for typography, negative space and refined contrast.

TRAVERTINE #CDB79B

Architectural neutral inspired by natural stone and the gelateria interior.

MUTED BRONZE #9A784A

Warm accent for signage, fine details and premium material references.

VISUAL LANGUAGE
A restrained palette, architectural neutrals and fine-line geometry shape the gelateria into a quiet, tactile experience.
