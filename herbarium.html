HERBARIUM

BRAND IDENTITY · PACKAGING · ART DIRECTION
BOTANICAL ARCHIVE / PIEMONTE, ITALIA
THE BRIEF

From cultivation to botanical archive.

Herbarium began as an agricultural project connected to lavender, medicinal herbs, infusions, essential oils and natural cosmetics.

The redesign transformed that identity into a contemporary botanical system built around collection, cataloguing and knowledge. The goal was to stay natural and delicate without falling into rustic clichés or the predictable visual language of traditional herbal brands.

01 / IDENTITY SYSTEMBOTANICAL ARCHIVE

The primary wordmark expresses the archival character of the project, while the monogram creates a compact secondary device for small-scale applications.

A restrained typographic hierarchy keeps the identity editorial rather than decorative, allowing the botanical material to become the expressive layer.

COLOUR SYSTEM

Quiet, botanical, contemporary.

Cerulean becomes the recognisable signature, softened by cloud cream and loden sage, while graphite anchors the system and preserves contrast.

BOTANICAL LANGUAGE

Illustration as classification.

The plant is treated as a specimen rather than a decorative motif. Botanical linework becomes the visual bridge between cultivation, scientific cataloguing and the finished product.

Lavandula angustifolia is presented through a precise archival language that can expand naturally across future species and product families.

PACKAGING SYSTEM

Specimen No. 004

A pack designed like an archive entry.

The packaging combines product information, botanical classification and illustration within a modular system. Each side has a clear function, while the visual language remains consistent around the entire pack.

PACKAGING ARTWORKFRONT · SIDES · BACK
DIGITAL EXPERIENCE

The archive becomes a shop.

The same language extends into a digital storefront without losing its editorial structure. Product discovery remains connected to specimen classification, botanical information and archival logic.
